Program filters

The program controls retain the selected district and use bookmarkable URLs. Unit Review follows unit type. Historical youth uses exact program rows; Venturing and Sea Scouts unit counts remain combined in the source and are unavailable separately. Persistence uses baseline Cub Scouts or Scouts BSA people, with endpoint presence anywhere in the council. Program cohorts may overlap; they must not be summed. Overview commissioner coverage has no verified program breakdown and is available only in Council view.

Historical sources

The Registration Guidebook, August 24, 2026, printed page 44 confirms that Monthly Membership, District Totals and Objectives Progress can be regenerated with historical date filters. Printed pages 4–5 define crossover, new member and registration categories.

Membership Tools also exposes five-year membership and unit trends, Registration Status by Month (2021–2026 choices), and monthly filters reaching December 2020. Available choices do not prove complete coverage for every period. The Historical Trends page contains ten December year-end reports (2016–2025), three completed 2026 quarter-ends and October 2025/2026 references. Earlier tested archive zeros are excluded. Older Exploring council totals exceed the exported district rows in some periods; these residuals are disclosed, never silently assigned to districts.

Historical unit-expire rosters are a separate permanent source. The guidebook warns that some advancement reports cannot be recreated later; a current rank profile cannot substitute for historical Arrow of Light completion.

Scout’s membership database

Scout’s DB preserves approved roster snapshots and supports identity-based comparisons, unit registrations and member movement. Concurrent registrations must be preserved for unit analysis and deduplicated for council people. Council movement, same-unit persistence and program transition are different outcomes.

No DB import, annual cohort or new crossover percentage is implied by this portal update. Its Unit Review page reads the current canonical Council Dashboard bundles; historical reports retain their own dates and population.

Commissioner workflow

Begin with a unit’s size and membership change, then review recruiting, leadership coverage, assignments, training and renewal outreach. Use the evidence to guide a conversation with the unit. Missing or unavailable data should prompt verification, not an inferred vacancy or negative outcome.
